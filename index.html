How it works

From plans to presentation in four steps.

  1. 01

    Start with the question

    Tell me what you sell, who you sell it to and where the conversation gets difficult.

  2. 02

    Send your plans

    PDF, DWG or a dimensioned sketch. You get a fixed scope, a quote and dates before work starts.

  3. 03

    Approve the model

    Check the 3D model against your drawings before a single image is made.

  4. 04

    Present

    Images, films and interactive files, sized for your meetings, your website and your clients' own devices.
